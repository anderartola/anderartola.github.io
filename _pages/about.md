Hi! I'm Ander, a PhD. student at the [Max Planck Institute for Software Systems](https://www.mpi-sws.org), under the supervision of [Dr. Manuel Gomez Rodriguez](https://people.mpi-sws.org/~manuelgr/) and as part of the IMPRS-TRUST program. Previously, I obtained my masters in Physics at Heidelberg University under the supervision of [Dr. Sarah Bosman](https://sites.google.com/view/sarahbosman). I graduated in 2023 from the Complutense University of Madrid in Mathematics and Theoretical Physics.



Some of my interests include:
* Game theory and strategic decision-making
* Generative AI markets: pricing, incentives, mechanism design and auditing
* Sequential decision-making: reinforcement learning, online learning and optimization
* Bayesian modelling, inference and optimization
* Game-theoretic statistics, anytime-valid inference and hypothesis testing
* Uncertainty quantification, explainability and support systems

Earlier work:
* Differential geometry and Lie groups
* Cosmology, dark matter, the intergalactic medium and reionisation
